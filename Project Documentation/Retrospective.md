# RETROSPECTIVES
After each sprint you will have to reflect on the previous sprint.
- You will discuss the process of the sprint. 
- It is NOT about the product, but about the process.
- The goal is to improve the process for the next sprint.

## Retrospective Individually [Sem] [06-10-2026]
### Sprint rating + why

### What went well?

### What didn't go well?

### What would you change?

### What would you keep?

### Biggest difficulty/stress?

### What should you improve next sprint?
